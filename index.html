About

Meet L.A.

Lori Ann Pope has spent 20 years turning rooms full of strangers into events people actually remember. Colleagues call her L.A. — which, fittingly, is also the name behind her company, l.a.pope, llc. She's an experiential marketer, producer, and strategist who's just as comfortable running a 15,000-person Expo as she is curating an intimate room of 100 C-suite executives — and she's led the cross-functional teams behind both without breaking a sweat.

Over nine years, she built the Boost small business training and education program at Facebook from the ground up. Her process is start to finish: Brainstorm to breakdown, content to agenda, agency partnerships to onsite execution. L.A. is hands-on at every step.

Here's her not-so-secret secret: her real superpower is building people. She just happens to build exceptional event programs along the way.

It all comes back to one thing — creating inclusive experiences that put people first and deliver results that are measurable, memorable, and meaningful.
