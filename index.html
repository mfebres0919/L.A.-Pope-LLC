About

Meet L.A.

Lori Ann Pope has spent 20 years turning rooms full of strangers into experiences people actually remember. Colleagues call her L.A., which, fittingly, is also the name behind her company, L.A. POPE, LLC. She's an experiential marketer, producer, and strategist who's just as comfortable running a 15,000-person conference and expo, as she is curating an intimate room of 100 C-suite executives: She's led the cross-functional teams behind both without breaking a sweat.

Over eight years, she built Boost, the SMB training and community engagement program at Facebook and Meta, from the ground up. Watching that work ripple through local communities around the globe—seeing economic development happen up close—was some of the most resonant work of her career. Her process is start to finish: Brainstorm to breakdown, content to agenda, agency partnerships to onsite execution. L.A. is hands-on at every step.

Here's her not-so-secret secret: her real superpower is building people. She just happens to build exceptional event programs along the way.

It all comes back to one thing — creating inclusive experiences that put people first and deliver results that are measurable, memorable, and meaningful.
